Privacy Policy: Munch the Frog

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Munch the Frog ("the app") is a children's game made by an independent developer in Australia ("we").

The short version

The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no chat and no links out of the app, and it does not connect to the internet.

What the app stores

The app saves your child's game progress (stars, levels played, chosen outfits, sound settings) only on the device, in the app's private storage. This information never leaves the device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or using "Hold to reset" in the parent area, deletes it.

Permissions

The app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Purchases

The app may offer one optional one-time purchase ("Unlock everything"), shown only to grown-ups behind a parent gate. If you buy it, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play on the device whether the purchase has been made.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and their families. It follows Google Play's Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to review, change or delete on our side.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
